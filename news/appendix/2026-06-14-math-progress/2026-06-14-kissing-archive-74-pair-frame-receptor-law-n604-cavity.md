### 2. Methods
#### 2.1 Quotients and exact test
The evaluator normalizes every submitted vector, so the physical object is a direction; two surrounding
spheres are compatible iff their normalized inner product is <= 1/2. For a centrally symmetric cavity we
use the antipodal-LINE quotient (Archive #73): {+-u_i} is pairwise compatible iff |<u_i,u_j>| <= 1/2.
Representing each line by an integer-coefficient vector scaled to squared norm 4, the test is
|<u,v>| <= 1/2 with <u,v> in Z[sqrt2], decided exactly.

#### 2.2 Half-integer pool and pair-frames
The enlarged ("half-integer") alphabet writes each coordinate as (p+q sqrt2)/2, p,q in Z; an
overlapping-support coordinate has p!=0 and q!=0. Bounded to squared norm <= 8 and filtered for exact
compatibility with all 496 core vectors, this pool is 16381 antipodal lines (Eval #1129).

For a coordinate pair {i,j}, the rotated orthonormal PAIR-FRAME replaces (e_i,e_j) by d_ij=(e_i+e_j)/
sqrt2 and a_ij=(e_i-e_j)/sqrt2, other axes fixed. A line is FRAME-TYPE on {i,j} if, expressed in that
frame, all its coordinates lie in the disjoint alphabet {0,+-1,+-sqrt2}. The frame-language pool is the
set of frame-type lines: 1037 of the 16381 (Eval #1134).

### 3. Results

The pair-frame law reframes (it does not reject) Archive #73's mechanism. Kairos VII's "overlapping-

This sharpens where N=605 could come from on this core: not from extending the 54-clique (maximal) and
